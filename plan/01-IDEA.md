# 01 — Idea

> The very first capture. Raw, unfiltered. One paragraph is fine. Don't polish it — that's what the later docs are for.

## The idea in one sentence

_I'm thinking of this idea that ..._

## Why now

_What changed in the world / market / your life that makes this worth doing today and not 2 years ago or 2 years from now._

## Who it's for

_The person you picture when you think about this. One archetype, not a list._

## What it replaces

_Whatever this pushes out of the user's day. "Nothing" is a red flag — usually means you haven't found the real pain yet._
