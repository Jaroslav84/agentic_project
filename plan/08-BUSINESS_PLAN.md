# Job AI — Business Plan

> *The commercial layer of the skilled trades ecosystem.*

---

## Executive Summary

Job AI is a vertical AI-powered workforce platform built exclusively for the skilled trades industry. It connects four stakeholders — job seekers, trade schools, employers, and brand advertisers — through a single AI-driven ecosystem. Unlike horizontal job boards (Indeed, ZipRecruiter) or narrow trade hiring tools (Skillit), Job AI owns the entire pipeline: from candidate discovery and AI-guided onboarding, through embedded certifications and verified matching, all the way to brand lifecycle advertising.

The founding team brings 20+ years of direct industry relationships across concrete, HVAC, asphalt, tree work, and electrical trades — including existing school and employer funnels that no competitor can replicate by building software alone.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

The skilled trades system is broken across all four stakeholder groups:

| Stakeholder | Pain |
|---|---|
| **Job Seekers** | No guidance, no feedback, no career path. Resume doesn't show real capability. |
| **Employers** | Buried under unqualified resumes. Manual screening. Expensive mis-hires. |
| **Trade Schools** | High student acquisition costs. Unqualified leads. No pre-vetting pipeline. |
| **Brand Advertisers** | Zero precision targeting to trade-specific buyers. Wasted ad impressions. |

### The Numbers
- **500,000+** unfilled trade jobs in the US right now
- **2.1 million** manufacturing and construction jobs projected to go unfilled by 2030
- **663,500** trade job openings expected every single year
- For every **5 Boomers** retiring, only **2** younger workers are entering the trades
- Over **22%** of US manufacturing facilities are operating below capacity due to labor shortage
- The economic impact of unfilled positions could exceed **$1 trillion by 2030**

## 4. Product & Technology

### AI Avatar & Candidate Journey

The platform is anchored by a lifelike, conversational AI avatar. Candidates don't log into a system — they interact with it. The avatar:
- Speaks, listens, and responds in real time
- Builds a complete workforce profile through conversation
- Guides the candidate step-by-step through certifications
- Evaluates behavioral readiness alongside technical skills

**Key differentiator:** This is not a chatbot layered on a form. It's a genuine interaction that produces a richer, more accurate profile than any resume.

### Certification Integration
- CPR / First Aid
- OSHA 10 and OSHA 30
- Trade-specific certifications (HVAC R-22, electrical licensing, etc.)
- State-specific compliance — platform knows what's required in each state

### Matching Engine & Gap Reports
- AI evaluates skill alignment between candidate profile and job requirements
- Match Score (0–100%) visible to both employer and candidate
- Gap Report shows exactly what skills/certs are missing and how to get them
- Behavioral readiness scoring alongside technical evaluation

### Advertiser Engine
- Lifecycle user data: trade interest at 16, certification at 18, job hunt at 20
- Precision CPM targeting by trade type, career stage, geography
- Zero wasted impressions — all targets are verified, progressing individuals
- First-mover brand loyalty at the moment of career decision
